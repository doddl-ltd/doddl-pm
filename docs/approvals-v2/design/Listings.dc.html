How this works

  1. The Listing agent keeps the should-be-live copy for every product and market, built from the product fact sheet in doddl HQ Knowledge.
  2. Every morning it reads each live listing from Amazon and compares field by field.
  3. A gap becomes a card: put ours back, or accept Amazon’s version as the new should-be-live.
  4. New copy you ask for goes through the Brand checker and claims check, then to you on the listing screen.
  5. After your yes it is sent to Amazon, shown as “on its way”, and checked again 24 hours later.
