How it sends

Sent through HubSpot sequences, from a separate outreach address
Pace 20 a day in week 1, then 50 a day
Days Tuesday to Thursday, 09:30 to 11:30 UK
Stops when they reply, opt out, bounce or book a call

Why not Klaviyo for this? Klaviyo is for people who signed up to hear from doddl. Its rules don’t allow emailing lists we found ourselves, and a breach could put the customer email account at risk. Nurseries who reply and want to hear more can be added to Klaviyo with their permission.
